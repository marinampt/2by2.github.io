is a collaborative art practice initiated by artists Marina Menendez-Pidal Trittin and Benjamin Niznik, dedicated to collecting, preserving, and reimagining the curiosities of the night through graphic design, print, performance, and public space.

Working in pairs is central to our methodology. We use night walking, collecting, drawing, writing, and making as ways of observing and responding to the environments around us, allowing ideas to develop through exchange rather than individual authorship. Our projects often begin as open-ended investigations and take shape through publications, objects, performances, and interventions in public space. We are interested in overlooked spaces, nocturnal environments, and the everyday traces that reveal how people inhabit and shape their surroundings.
